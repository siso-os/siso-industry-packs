## Why this is not the answer yet

1. **Shallow evidence.** The readers ran about one search per firm; most scores are 0 for lack of
   looking, not lack of signal. Pass 3 (hand pass, one firm at a time) fixes the top 40.
2. **Misfits in the list.** Some rows are a course seller (a Gumroad domain) or a general talent
   firm, not an OFM agency. Pass 3 removes them.
3. **The list is too short.** 120 candidates against a ~500 target. Desirely alone lists 431;
   expansion is running.
4. **Self-reported figures dominate.** "400 creators", "$35M revenue" are the firms' own claims,
   scored down per the rubric.
