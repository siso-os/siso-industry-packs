# C Home case: HALO

The yardstick for this pack: SISO's client HALO, a creator agency, mapped on the same anatomy
fields as the top firms (T4) so T7 and T8 can say where it is ahead and behind. Business level
only, from SISO's notes (11 Oct 2026); no code, no personal data, no figures. Its software lives
in its own repositories, not here.

Shaan (11 Oct): "we've got a cross streaming agency, we've got people who bring in models, we've
got like at some point we're going to do content generation for them, we might do ads."

## Anatomy

| Field | HALO |
|---|---|
| Offer | OFM management plus **cross-platform live streaming**: a creator goes live once and the agency broadcasts to several cam platforms at the same time |
| Revenue lines | Commission on fan-platform earnings; streaming takes and tips; referral bounties; planned: content generation, ads |
| Client acquisition | **Recruiters / referrers who bring in models**, paid by referral bounty |
| Traffic acquisition | Live streaming itself is the traffic engine (cam platforms' own audiences); ads planned; social traffic not yet mapped |
| Departments and roles | Creators; chatters (fan messages, custom requests); streaming operators (run live shows from an operator board); managers (onboarding, account status, payout questions); HR/finance (staff pay, contractor invoices, contracts); admin/VAs (automation, tasks, recruiting) |
| Operating loop | Invite → register → platform accounts connected → contract signed → go live / post → chat and customs → earnings recorded → creator statement and payout → staff pay |
| Money model | Creator earnings held as money owed to the creator (a liability), commission as agency revenue; per-creator statements |
| Tools (by job) | Own CRM (creators, onboarding, contracts, invoicing, payroll, customs tracker); own streaming system (creator app + operator board + multistream server); team chat; open-source donor apps for CRM base, tasks and docs; workflow automation; AI voice for promo content |
| Contracts, pricing, pay | Not recorded here (private to the client) |

## Against a typical OFM agency (first read; T7 tests it with counts)

| Ahead | Behind |
|---|---|
| Back office that the OFM tool vendors do not sell: contracts, invoicing, payroll, payouts | The fan layer that every vendor sells: unified inbox, AI chat, per-chatter revenue attribution |
| Multistreaming to several cam platforms from one go-live, run by an operator | Chatter QA and the measurement under staff gamification |
| Referral network for bringing in creators | Analytics on real data; social traffic (Reddit, X, TikTok, IG) not yet a department |
