# OnlyFans management agencies: summary

**11 Oct 2026 · first full pass of the top-down deep dive · HALO is the home case**

**Verdict** `[analysis]`: build on HALO, in this order:
1. A real ledger with live creator statements, plus **Creator Career** (tiers, locked splits,

## The five findings

1. **An agency is five machines:** attention, conversation, inventory, supply and trust. About
   70% of fan money comes through conversation (DMs and pay-per-view), only ~4% through
   subscriptions. [P](P-physics.md)
2. **The average managed creator barely pays for a human chat team.** At a 35% share, break-even
   is ~$5.6k gross a month with human chatters and ~$2.4k with AI drafts and human closers. That
   roughly doubles to triples the creators worth managing. [T8](T8-first-principles.md)
3. **Retention is the valuation.** Agencies sell for ~2× annual profit because creators can walk.
   No profiled agency gives creators progression, tenure economics or live statements. [R](R-retention.md)
4. **HALO's rarest assets are the moat:** cross-platform live streaming (2 of 21 visible top
   agencies offer any streaming) and a recruiter network (1 of 21 has a recruiting department).
   Its gamification engine is built, but for chatters, not creators. [T7](T7-analysis.md) · [C](C-case-halo.md)
5. **AI is good enough for photos and voice, close for short video.** The binding constraint is
   consent and labelling, and nobody runs a per-asset consent ledger. [A](A-ai-content.md)
