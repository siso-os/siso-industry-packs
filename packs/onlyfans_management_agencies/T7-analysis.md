## What it says

1. **The industry's common core is chat + account management + leak protection.** HALO has the
   first two in people, the leak service not at all; it is cheap to add (a vendor or perceptual
   hashing + takedowns).
2. **Traffic is everyone's weak, informal department.** Even the visible top name Reddit/TikTok
   in under half of cases. A real traffic machine is a differentiator, not table stakes.
3. **HALO's two rarest assets are its moat:** live cross-streaming (2 of 21) and a recruiter
   network (1 of 21 has a recruiting department).
4. **Transparency is rare** (7 of 21 publish a rate) and correlates with the most-reviewed
   firms (SirenCY, Bunny): the cheap trust play from [R](R-retention.md).
5. **AI is barely visible** (3 of 21) despite photos and voice being good enough
   ([A](A-ai-content.md)): an open lane.
