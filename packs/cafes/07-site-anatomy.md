# Site anatomy: cafés

Written by SISO-AGENCY (reader), 7 Oct 2026, for UI-HUB. Cafés are the first site we ship (Café 89, Đà Nẵng). This is the **need side**: what a café site must do, slot by slot, how often real sites do it, which variants are worth building, what each slot does when an unclaimed café has only a Google Maps listing and a delivery-app page, and which rules bind it. It is a cafe cut of the restaurants pack (`packs/restaurants/07-site-anatomy.md`, 41 slots), same columns and same `slots.json` shape, with café-only slots added and restaurant-only slots turned off. Machine twin: `slots.json` (47 slots; pages: Global 6, Home 14, Menu 8, Our place 6, Visit 6, Optional: Reservations 1, Optional: Events and groups 1, Optional: Gift cards 1, Optional: Locations 1, Optional: Loyalty 1, Optional: Shop 1, Optional: Careers 1).
Tags: **[data]** counted by us; **[research]** a published source; **[reasoned]** our inference or an ADR.
Field names: `business.*` are in the frozen `site.json/1`. Every other field (`photos[]`, `hours`, `rating`, `reviews[]`, `menu.*`, `contact.*`, `delivery.*`, `attributes[]`, `copy.*`) is a **proposed name** for SISO-SITES to confirm in the enrichment schema; new café fields are `hours.opens_early`, `menu.groups[]`, `menu.items[].sizes[]`, `menu.items[].temp`, `menu.combos[]`, `menu.flat_price`, `attributes[wifi, laptop_friendly, power_outlets, ac, seating_*, takeaway, dine_in, delivery, parking, payments]`.

## 2. The sitemap

**The café ships four pages and one button.** Home, Menu, Our place, Visit. The rest of what real sites carry is either a section, a link-out, or off.

| Page type | Cafés (n/9) | Đà Nẵng live | Ship? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | 9 | all | Always |
| Visit: hours, map, contact | 7 show hours; 2 give directions | contact 76%, phone link 59%, Maps link 19% | Always (page at tier 2, section at tier 0/1) |
| Menu | 4 (Café 89, Kikas, Gail's, Prufrock) | 41% | Always: page at tier 2, section at tier 0/1 |
| Our place / about | 8 | 79% (loose) | Page only with a sourced story or 6+ photos; else a home section |
| Order / delivery | 1 (Gail's) | ShopeeFood link 5%, GrabFood 0% | A button and a band, never a page |
| Gallery | 0 as a page | 22% (loose) | Section only |
| FAQ | 1 | 19% | A home block from known facts, or the assistant |
| Shop / beans | at least 4 (roasters) | 38% (loose) | No (Catalogue module) |
| Loyalty / app | 2 | 26% (loose) | No (ADR 0011) |
| Locations | 4 (chains) | 62% | No |
| Events | 1 | 27% (loose) | Only if on the owner's own page |
| Reservations | 1 | 45% (loose; over-fires) | No for cafés |
| Gift cards, careers, newsletter, blog | not per café | 7%, 34%, -, 78% (loose) | No |

**True one-pagers:** Café 89 and Kikas (both ours). Tartine and Gail's are one long page; the roasters are retail sites with 6 to 9 top-level pages [data].

### Tiers: shape follows data richness [reasoned]

| Tier | What the café has | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| 0 Below the gate | under 3 own photos, or no hours, address, rating | One-pager: Hero, Essentials, What it's like (if attributes), Menu tiles or 'Ask on Zalo', Visit. `noindex`. Bar has 2 buttons. **This is where an unclaimed café with only Maps + Grab lands.** |
| 1 Passed the gate, thin | 3 to 5 own photos, 12 reviews, menu categories or up to 15 items | One-pager with 3 anchors (Menu, Our place, Visit): the Café 89 / Kikas shape |
| 2 Rich | 6+ own photos, 16+ items (some priced), 25+ reviews, a sourced story | Four pages: Home, Menu, Our place, Visit; gallery becomes a home section |
| 3 Roaster or bakery | beans or bakery counter, its own shop | Tier 2 plus a link-out to the shop (no cart) |

### When a page collapses into a home section
- **Menu becomes the home Featured menu** when the menu has fewer than 16 items **and** fewer than 4 categories. Café 89 (about a dozen drinks, 4 chips) stays a section; a juice bar with 40 items and a bánh mì group becomes a page.
- **Our place becomes the home Story teaser plus What it's like** when sourced story is under 3 sentences or own photos are under 6.
- **Visit never collapses to nothing.** It is at minimum the Location teaser, Hours and the action bar's Directions button.
- **Gallery is always a section.** At 3 to 5 photos it folds into Hero and What it's like.
- **Order is never a page.** One band, two buttons.

## 3. How a café differs from a restaurant

| Café-specific | What it changes in the slots |
|---|---|
| **Drink-led menu** | Menu groups are drink-first (cà phê, trà, nước ép, sinh tố, soda), then bánh mì / bakery / food. Hot-iced and S/M/L are markers on one row, not separate items. Vietnamese names stay (cà phê muối, bạc xỉu). A flat-price board (juice 20k) is a real pattern: the **deal ribbon** in Specials. |
| **Opens early** | The question is "is it open now, and since when?" Open-now chip with 'Mở cửa 6:30' while closed, in Essentials, Hours, the bar and the footer. One tiny inline script (<1 KB) with a static fallback. |
| **Wifi, laptop, work** | A **Work and wifi** slot (Café 89's 'Làm việc hay thư giãn' card), from Maps attributes only. Never a wifi password or speed. The assistant answers 'is there wifi?' from the same field. |
| **Takeaway and grab-and-go** | The **Takeaway and delivery band**: takeaway chip from a Maps service option, Grab and ShopeeFood link-outs. No cart, no checkout. |
| **Seating and vibe** | **What it's like** and **Space and seating** carry shade, corner, floor seating, rooftop, pets, big screen. Restaurants have one 'ambience' line. |
| **Bánh mì, juice, bakery shapes** | Short lists, combos, flat prices. The **Food and bánh mì group** slot appears only when the menu photo or Grab listing shows food. A drink-only café never shows an empty tab. |
| **No reserve, no gift cards** | Reservations, gift cards, events-as-dining, careers, loyalty, newsletter are off (R17, R19 of the restaurants pack). |
| **Cart-free** | restaurant-app's Add, BottomSheet-with-cart, StickyOrderCTA, Orders and Loyalty pages are dropped. The Café 89 drink-dialog is the read-only equivalent. |

### The Vietnam overlay (ADR 0008), with numbers
- **Contact:** Zalo deep link, then phone, then Messenger. Local bar: Facebook 68%, phone link 59%, Zalo 31% of live sites. **0 of the 19 outlines (including ours) shows Zalo.**
- **Order:** GrabFood and ShopeeFood hold over 90% of delivery, yet **GrabFood links on 0% and ShopeeFood on 5%** of live Đà Nẵng café sites. Buttons only with real store links; a Grab destination link beside Google directions. An easy wow for the owner.
- **Language:** Vietnamese, English and Korean for Đà Nẵng (Korea is the largest foreign source, to confirm). A language switch exists on 27% of live sites (25% of independents, 67% of chains). Text is tokenised, translated once at build time.
- **Dead sites:** 48% of café sites listed in Overture do not load (245 are dead domains). Those cafés once paid for a site and are warmer leads (`dead_gbp`). The local design bar is low: mostly hand-built WordPress, almost no site builders.
- **Payments:** user-needs-by-page lists GoPay, OVO, ShopeePay for Bali. In Vietnam the equivalents are VietQR transfer, MoMo, ZaloPay, cards, cash [reasoned]; shown only from Maps attributes or the owner.
- **Parking:** motorbike parking and the hẻm (alley) address matter more than the street number.

## 5. Rules attached to slots (café set, C1 to C18)

## 6. A slot is a block: the contract

Each slot above is a block under `block-contract-v1` (an `ui_only`, read-only block; no side effects, no tenants, no authority). Mapping, so UI-HUB declares them consistently:

| Contract field | In this pack |
|---|---|
| `id`, `kind` | `cafe.<slot>@<version>`, kind `component`; one id per variant (N1, H5, `menu-chips`...) |
| `ports.data` | the slot's `fields`, mode `read_only`, source of truth `site.json` |
| `ports.ui` states | **ready** (full data), **thin** (the `thin_data` form), **empty** (`empty_state`: show / fold / hide). Loading, error, denied, stale are not rendered on a static site. |
| `ports.actions` | none, except link-outs (Zalo, Grab, Maps, `tel:`), each with `external-effect: true`, approval none (a link) |
| ui accessibility | touch targets at least 44x44 pt; visible focus; semantic headings; reduced motion (user-needs-by-page) |
| token refs | colour, type, radius, spacing from the site's token set (ADR 0004); nothing hard-coded |
| `evidence` | the slot's `evidence` and `prevalence` |
| admission | `not_admitted` until a Lighthouse mobile check (ADR 0016) and a thin-data render pass |

## 7. What the best do that most don't, and what nobody does but should

**The three design-led cafés do, and most don't**
1. **Keep the home short, let photos carry it** (Prufrock: four blocks). Real small cafés stack 6 to 8 [data].
2. **Put one honest line of vibe at the top** (Monmouth, Prufrock). We write ours; never a claim.
3. **Story as proof**: farms, producers, roasters (Monmouth, Ozone, Blue Bottle). Only if the owner states it.

**What nobody does, but the evidence says we should**
1. **The Vietnam overlay**: Zalo-first bar, Grab and ShopeeFood buttons. 0 of 9 cafés, 0% GrabFood, 5% ShopeeFood in Đà Nẵng [data].
2. **An essentials strip with open-now and early opening.** 0 of 9; 64% of customers check hours and location, only 8.6% of Istanbul sites show hours [data; research].
3. **Amenities from the listing** (wifi, laptop, shade, parking). 0 of 9 as a block; 8 of 10 trial cafés had them [data].
4. **A real thin-data form for every slot.** Every platform and every study assumes a full client. Ours must look finished at 3 photos and 12 reviews.
5. **Price with its source line.** 0 of 19.
6. **Both widths as equals**, key actions in the header on desktop and in the bar on phone. Only 2 of 9 cafés (ours) expose a bar.

## 8. What the old data already settled, and what is still open

**Settled (do not redo)**
1. **The slot system exists.** restaurant-app's `template-variants.md` is slots x variants x data for hero, reviews, essentials, menu, story, map, CTA, gallery, specials, Instagram, About and Loyalty (OLD-DATA-MAP #1). This pack only filters it for cafés and adds the café-only slots; the **variants worth building** column names those ports first.
2. **The need side exists.** `user-needs-by-page.md` fixes goals and must-haves per page, Web Vitals (LCP 2.5 s, INP 200 ms, CLS 0.1) and 44x44 pt targets. Café keeps Home, Menu, Contact, About, Specials, Reviews; drops Orders, Loyalty, Reservations, Gift Cards, Blog.
3. **The shape:** four pages, sticky bar, HTML menu, static hero, ❤️ + SISO footer, VI/EN/KO, Zalo then phone then Messenger, Grab and ShopeeFood (ADRs 0007, 0008; UI-HUB round 2 accepted as base).
4. **The gate** (3 own photos, hours, address+map, rating, menu items or categories) and **the price rule**: sourced real prices shown with their source (ADR 0003 as revised 04:37), superseding the older 'no prices ever' wording.
5. **The variation machine**: personality to pools (N, H, O, F, type, motion) with a collision check (ADR 0004; VARIATION-ENGINE). The slots here are what those variants fill.
6. **The block schema** is `block-contract-v1` (section 6), not a new one.
7. **The Vietnam local bar** (Facebook 68, phone 59, Zalo 31, Instagram 30, language switch 27, ShopeeFood 5, GrabFood 0, menu page 41, PDF 3).

**Still open**
1. **Where a café's own photos come from.** Maps photos by strangers are not usable (trial: own photos 0 of 10); the owner's Facebook or Instagram trial is not run. This decides the gate pass rate more than anything else.
2. **Menu items.** 9 of 10 trial cafés had none on Maps. Menu-photo reading works for roughly 1 café in 3 (Haiku finds the photo, a strong model reads it at 1,440 px, a second pass checks); a price from a photo carries `price_source` and a date.
3. **Whether the new café slots earn their keep**: Work and wifi, Space and seating, Takeaway band, Food and bánh mì group, Item dialog. Reasoned from Café 89 and the listing attributes; test in the first 10.
4. **Counts are floors on 9 cafés** (3 of them retail roasters, 3 rendered thin). A rendered-DOM pass on the JS sites and a count of the 100 overseas cafés from UI-HUB's page would tighten them.
5. **Korean:** the Korean-visitor share and copy quality (ADR 0008 'to confirm').
6. **Grab destination link format** (SISO-SITES tests it), and whether Vietnamese customers prefer Grab or Shopee per area.
7. **Open-now needs a script.** Decided here: one inline script under 1 KB with a static fallback. UI-HUB confirms it fits the 200 KB / 95 budget.
8. **Payments in Vietnam** (VietQR, MoMo, ZaloPay) are reasoned, not counted.
9. **Mid-range Android share** of Vietnamese visitors, from ADR 0015 analytics once live.
10. **Ariker 2012's 29-attribute list** was never obtained.
